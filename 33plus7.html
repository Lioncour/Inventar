About 33+7

The 33+7 capsule wardrobe system is a minimalist approach to fashion that emphasizes quality over quantity and intentional choices over impulse purchases.

The Rules

33 Core Pieces: Essential items that form the foundation of your wardrobe for 3 months. These are versatile, high-quality pieces that work together seamlessly.

7 Seasonal Additions: Items that rotate throughout the year based on weather, occasions, or personal preference. These keep your wardrobe fresh and adaptable.

Total: 40 Items Maximum - This constraint forces intentional choices and reduces decision fatigue while maximizing style and versatility.

This system promotes sustainable fashion, reduces waste, and helps you build a wardrobe that truly reflects your personal style.
